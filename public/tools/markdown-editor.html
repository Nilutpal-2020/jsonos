Frequently Asked Questions

Can I render Mermaid diagrams?

Yes, just create a fenced code block with the 'mermaid' language identifier. Flowcharts, sequence diagrams, and more will render live.

How does the KaTeX math work?

Wrap your LaTeX math equations in $...$ for inline math or $$...$$ for block math, and they will render instantly.

Are my markdown documents saved?

Yes, your documents are auto-saved locally to your browser's IndexedDB. They are completely private and never uploaded.
