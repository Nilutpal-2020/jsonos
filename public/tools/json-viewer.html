How to view JSON online

  1. Open the viewer and paste your JSON data or drag-and-drop a .json file.
  2. Ensure the workspace is set to Tree view to see your data visualized as expandable nodes.
  3. Click arrows to expand or collapse objects and arrays.
  4. Use the search bar to find specific keys or values deep within the structure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I view JSON as a tree?

Simply paste your JSON data into the editor and switch to the 'Tree' view. The tool will automatically parse and display your data as interactive, collapsible nodes.

Can this viewer handle large JSON files?

Yes. Our tree view is highly optimized and virtualized, allowing it to easily handle and render very large JSON documents without freezing your browser.

Is my data uploaded to your servers?

No. The JSON viewer runs completely locally in your browser. We do not upload, store, or process your data on any external servers.
